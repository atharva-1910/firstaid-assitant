## Safety rules marked [VERIFY] (backend/app/safety_rules.py)

- Scorpion sting: clinic or hospital at minimum
- Dog bite: clinic the same day; emergency if bleeding will not stop, deep, or on the face or neck
- Chemical burn: emergency for eyes, face, a large area, or an acid attack
- Thermal burn: emergency if larger than the palm, on face, neck, hands, feet, genitals or joints, or with breathing difficulty
- Unknown bite: treated as a possible snakebite (emergency) and shows the snakebite steps until the type is known
- "Not sure" about breathing: treated as not breathing normally (Path A)

## Myth set

- `myth_dog_chilli`: Putting chilli powder, turmeric or oil on a dog bite — reason to verify: Does not clean the wound; washing with soap and water does.
- `myth_dog_ignore`: Ignoring a small dog bite or scratch — reason to verify: Even small bites can transmit rabies.
- `myth_burn_paste`: Putting toothpaste, butter, oil or ghee on a burn — reason to verify: It traps heat and raises the risk of infection.
- `myth_burn_ice`: Putting ice on a burn — reason to verify: It can damage the skin further.
- `myth_chem_neutralise`: Neutralising an acid burn with another substance — reason to verify: It can cause more damage; rinse with water instead.
- `myth_elec_touch`: Touching a person still in contact with electricity — reason to verify: You can be electrocuted too.
- `myth_unr_water`: Giving water to an unresponsive person — reason to verify: They cannot swallow safely.
- `myth_unr_onion`: Making an unconscious person smell onions or shoes — reason to verify: It does not help and wastes time.
- `myth_ha_gas`: Treating chest pain as gas and waiting — reason to verify: Delay can cost a life; call 112.
- `myth_choking_water`: Giving water to a choking person — reason to verify: It can block the airway further.
